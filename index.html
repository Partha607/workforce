03 — Verified Attendance

Dual-layer biometric check-in.

A shift only counts when two things are true at once: the live face matches the enrolled identity, and GPS is inside the site's geo-fence. Anything else is flagged, blocked, or escalated automatically.

  • ✓

  • ✓

    Strict or flexible per site — block off-site, or allow with sign-off.

06 — Payroll Engine
The hardest part, solved

Payroll that's compliant by default.

Approved attendance is the single source of truth. The engine runs a strict, ordered pipeline and produces identical results every time. Same inputs, same payslips.

  • ₹
    Statutory built in — EPF, ESI, PT and TDS against versioned, year-specific rules.
  • ₹
    Real-world pay — overtime, night shifts, allowances, LOP, bonuses, arrears, advances, loans.
  • ₹
    Old & new tax regimes — annualised, per-employee TDS with rebates and cess.
  • ₹
    Exception guardrails — missing details or anomalies block the run until resolved.
